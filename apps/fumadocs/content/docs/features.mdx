## Portfolio overview

The dashboard consolidates current assets into the user's base currency and shows net worth, liquidity, allocation, valuation coverage and Indian-equity performance. It surfaces missing FX rates instead of presenting incomplete totals as complete.

## Analytics workspace

The dedicated Analytics page keeps the dashboard concise while combining account-owned wealth, liquidity, tradebook, salary, household and property trends. EvilCharts/ECharts blocks use interactive legends, precise currency tooltips and container-aware resizing for phones and larger screens. Each section appears only when its underlying dataset exists; charts never use sample data.

## Cash and deposits

- **INR and EUR accounts** are separate cash views. Only masked account identifiers and dated balance snapshots are stored.
- **Fixed deposits** track principal, rate, term, compounding, status and maturity. Each update preserves the previous snapshot.

EUR bank cash is intentionally separate from Degiro investments and broker activity.

## Listed investments

- **Indian investments** combine Zerodha holdings and tradebooks without mixing their roles. Holdings create immutable current-valuation snapshots. Users can select up to ten annual tradebooks; the browser processes them sequentially and overlapping trades are deduplicated across files.
- Tradebook intelligence shows monthly purchases and redemptions, net invested cash, financial-year coverage, contribution consistency, fund concentration and history-quality gaps. Realized return, CAGR and XIRR stay hidden until every redemption has sufficient earlier acquisition history.
- **Global equity** imports Degiro Transactions and Account CSV exports. The application reconstructs open quantities, moving-average cost basis, realized results, dividends, fees and recent activity from the retained ledger.

Global-equity valuations are based on the latest imported trade price and are explicitly labelled as estimates, not live quotes.

## Physical and manual assets

- **Commodities** separate each declared, valued holding from its physical item inventory. Items track quantity/unit, gross weight, optional purity, ownership, custody, provenance, appraisal and dated history. Reconciliation shows itemized weight, declared weight and any gap.
- Physical items never create a second net-worth asset. They enter the FIRE corpus only after the user explicitly marks them eligible and supplies both a defensible valuation and liquidation factor.
- **Real estate** tracks property identity, owner, type, location, area, ownership share, legal status, unit price and full-market valuation. Analytics use the value attributable to the user's ownership share.
- **Manual assets** cover assets that do not have a dedicated model.

## Salary intelligence

- Bulk PDF selection supports up to 50 payslips and processes each file independently for reliable Vercel uploads.
- SHA-256 fingerprints prevent an identical payslip from being imported twice. Revised statements for the same month remain auditable while current analytics use the newest revision.
- Monthly earnings separate base salary, bonuses and special payments, taxable wage, wage tax, pension, social insurance, 30% ruling adjustments, reimbursements and deposited net pay.
- Reconciliation checks compare net-wage components and bank-transfer totals with the payable amount. A statement that does not reconcile is retained with a review warning rather than silently treated as verified.
- Trend charts, annual totals, typical monthly take-home and missing-period detection make incomplete salary history visible.

## Indian income-tax history

- Bulk JSON import supports official ITR-2 and ITR-3 exports across assessment years, with exact-file duplicate detection and preserved revisions.
- The application normalizes income heads, Chapter VI-A deductions, taxable income, liability, interest, advance tax, TDS, TCS, self-assessment tax, balance payable and refund due.
- Every import is arithmetically reconciled. Inconsistent returns stay visible with a review warning instead of being presented as verified.
- Annual charts show reported income composition and tax settlement history; gaps between imported assessment years are disclosed.
- This is a history of values declared in the ITR JSON. Government acceptance still requires the corresponding processing intimation when independent confirmation matters.

## Dutch income-tax history

- Bulk PDF import supports official **Definitieve aanslag inkomstenbelasting** and revised final-assessment notices across tax years. Exact files are deduplicated, while a genuinely changed notice for the same taxpayer and year remains an auditable revision.
- An assessment can belong to the account owner or a configured family member. Every record and import remains scoped to the signed-in account.
- The application normalizes accepted Box 1, Box 2 and Box 3 income and tax, social-insurance premiums, tax credits, payroll withholding, provisional refunds, tax interest and the final refund or amount payable.
- Arithmetic reconciliation verifies both the final tax calculation and the assessment settlement. A notice that does not reconcile remains visible with a review warning.
- Annual charts compare accepted income composition, final tax, withholding, credits and settlement. Current analytics use only the latest successful revision for each taxpayer and tax year.
- The PDF is parsed in memory and discarded. Names, addresses and the full assessment reference are not retained.

## Household costs

- **Monthly budget** separates gross expenses from government or other refunds, then shows the true household cost, annual run rate and per-adult share.
- **Scenario planning** compares the current budget with minimum-away, stress and custom cases using household-level arithmetic.
- **Service contracts** track current terms and cost history, flag expired or approaching end dates, and highlight differences from the matching budget line.
- **One-time purchases** keep house-setup, car and other capital spending separate from recurring living costs.
- Category and scenario charts expose the largest cost drivers and refund dependency without treating refunds as negative expenses.

## FIRE planning

- **Family planning** supports the account owner, partner, children and other dependants without hardcoded names. Portfolio-linked wealth and manually entered partner wealth are kept distinct to prevent double counting.
- **Phase-aware expenses** can start or stop in specific years, so education, childcare, loans and later-life care do not incorrectly continue forever.
- **Doable, Safety Max and custom scenarios** compare retirement dates, spending levels, inflation, returns and safety buffers against the same private source data.
- **Annual simulation** shows opening assets, returns, pre-retirement savings, retirement expenses, pensions or other income, one-time life events and closing assets for every year.
- **Auditable corpus breakdown** separates the recurring safe-withdrawal reserve from the retirement-date reserve for one-time life events, then compares their sum with the deterministic lifespan requirement and uses the higher result.
- **Risk ranges** run 1,000 reproducible return-and-inflation paths and show 10th-percentile, median and 90th-percentile outcomes alongside the expected path.
- **Essential-floor spending** protects essential costs while allowing a limited discretionary reduction when withdrawal pressure crosses the configured guardrail.

The model is designed for early-retirement horizons, not only a fixed 30-year retirement. The editable safe-withdrawal rate is a binding policy floor for recurring spending, not a guarantee or a decorative reference. The product approach is informed by [Morningstar's 2025 retirement-income research](https://www.morningstar.com/content/cs-assets/v3/assets/blt9415ea4cc4157833/bltb73b87c5d0c70ead/692f43f57737a31596684522/working_file_11.19_FINAL_REVISE.pdf) and [Vanguard's retirement-income principles](https://corporate.vanguard.com/content/dam/corp/research/pdf/vanguard_principles_retirement_income.pdf).

## Data management

- Import history with row counts, deduplication results and processing status.
- Account-scoped JSON export containing portfolio, salary, income-tax, household and FIRE records with relevant history.
- Archival that removes an item from current views without deleting its historical snapshots.
- User preferences for base currency, locale and time zone.
- Dated exchange rates for multi-currency aggregation.
