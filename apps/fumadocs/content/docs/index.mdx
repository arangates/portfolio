Selvam is a private, multi-currency portfolio application for consolidating cash, deposits, listed investments, commodities, real estate and other assets without losing historical records.

## Product principles

<IconCardGrid columns={2}>
  <IconCard
    icon="Fingerprint"
    title="Private by account"

  />
  <IconCard
    icon="FileClock"

  />
  <IconCard

## Current boundaries

<InfoPanel title="Scope and valuation limits" icon="ShieldCheck" tone="info">
  Selvam does not provide live security prices, trading, bank connectivity, tax calculations or
  investment advice. Global-equity market values are estimates from the latest imported Degiro trade
  price. EUR/INR conversion uses official ECB reference-rate history and can optionally use a fresh
  licensed intraday quote for current valuation; assets without FX coverage are excluded rather than
  silently assigned a rate.
</InfoPanel>
