Selvam is a **100% sovereign, local-first portfolio intelligence application**. It consolidates multi-currency cash, bank deposits, listed equities, mutual funds, commodities, real estate, and salary history without losing historical provenance or depending on third-party cloud services.

## Current boundaries

<InfoPanel title="Scope and sovereign operation" icon="ShieldCheck" tone="info">
  Selvam executes completely on your machine. Currency conversion uses official European Central
  Bank (ECB) daily reference rates fetched directly from the ECB XML feed without third-party API
  aggregators or API keys. Assets without FX coverage are explicitly disclosed rather than silently
  assigned guessed rates.
</InfoPanel>
