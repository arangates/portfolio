Selvam is a private, multi-currency portfolio application for consolidating cash, deposits, listed investments, commodities, real estate and other assets without losing historical records.

## Product principles

- **Account ownership is mandatory.** Every portfolio record belongs to one authenticated user; a new user begins with an empty portfolio.
- **History is append-only.** A balance or valuation update creates a dated snapshot instead of replacing the previous value.
- **Imports are repeatable.** Broker exports are retained and deduplicated so overlapping uploads do not duplicate activity or erase earlier data.
- **Source data remains authoritative.** Calculations distinguish imported facts from estimates, such as a global-equity valuation based on the latest imported trade price.
- **Secrets stay outside Git.** Credentials and personal source files are supplied through local or deployment environment variables.

## Current boundaries

Selvam does not provide live market prices, trading, bank connectivity, tax calculations or investment advice. Global-equity market values are estimates from the latest imported Degiro trade price. Exchange-rate coverage is user-managed, and assets without a required FX rate are excluded from converted totals rather than silently assigned a rate.
