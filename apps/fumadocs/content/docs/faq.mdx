## General & Account

### How do I activate my account?

When you register for Selvam, an activation email containing a secure one-time verification link is sent to your registered address. Clicking this link confirms your email address and grants access to your dashboard workspace. Verification links remain active for 24 hours.

### Can I change my registered email address?

Yes. Go to [Settings](/dashboard/settings) > **Account** to update your primary email address. A confirmation link will be delivered to both the old and new email addresses to prevent unauthorized changes.

### How does Selvam protect my financial data?

Selvam enforces bank-grade security standards:

- **256-bit encryption** in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256).
- **Read-only integrations**: Selvam never requests transaction or withdrawal permissions from your linked bank or brokerage accounts.
- **Strict isolation**: Multi-tenant database architecture backed by Lakebase Postgres ensures complete isolation of user data.

## Portfolio Tracking & Asset Classes

### Which financial assets can I track in Selvam?

Selvam provides unified tracking across:

- **Indian Equities & Stocks**: Live price sync via Kite and NSE/BSE feeds.
- **Mutual Funds & ETFs**: Direct growth funds, NAV updates, and SIP schedules.
- **Fixed & Term Deposits**: Bank deposits, interest rate comparisons, cumulative vs non-cumulative yields, and maturity payout reminders.
- **Commodities & Bullion**: Physical gold (24K, 22K), sovereign gold bonds (SGB), and silver bars.
- **Real Estate & Mortgages**: Property valuations, outstanding principal, and amortisation schedules.
- **Multi-Currency Cash**: Bank accounts in INR, EUR, and USD with automated FX conversion.

### Can I import past transactions via Excel or CSV?

Yes. Navigate to [Imports](/dashboard/imports) to upload standard bank statement workbooks, Zerodha tradebooks, or household ledger spreadsheets. Selvam automatically categorizes transactions and reconciles your historical net worth.

### How are portfolio returns and XIRR calculated?

Selvam calculates Extended Internal Rate of Return (XIRR) across all cash inflows, SIP deductions, dividend credits, and real-time market valuations to provide your true annualized return, taking timing of capital deployment into account.

## Notifications & Digests

### What automated email digests does Selvam deliver?

Selvam delivers structured, responsive notifications for:

- **Reminders Digest**: Consolidated overview of upcoming bills, FD maturities, and contract renewals.
- **Bill Payment Alerts**: Advance notice before credit card, utility, and recurring subscription due dates.
- **Fixed Deposit Maturities**: Payout schedules and prevailing bank interest rates for reinvestment.
- **Investment Updates**: Market movements, top gainers/losers, and dividend credits.
- **Contract Renewals**: Notice period warnings for leases, gym memberships, and insurance policies.
- **Currency Triggers**: Target exchange rate alerts for EUR/INR remittances.
- **FIRE Milestones**: Celebrations when your corpus crosses major financial independence benchmarks.

### How do I customize alert frequency or delivery channels?

Visit [Notification Preferences](/dashboard/settings) to customize:

- Email digest frequency (Daily, Weekly on Mondays, or Monthly).
- Thresholds for urgent bill alerts (e.g., 7 days, 3 days, or on due date).
- Web Push notification toggle.

### What should I do if Selvam emails land in Spam or Promotions?

To ensure uninterrupted delivery of time-sensitive financial reminders:

1. Add `notifications@selvam.app` to your email contact list or VIP senders.
2. If using Gmail, drag the email from the **Promotions** tab to the **Primary** tab and click **Yes** when asked to deliver future messages to Primary.

## Cross-Border & Tax

### How does multi-currency conversion work?

Selvam continuously tracks live foreign exchange rates between EUR, INR, and USD. You can set target rate alerts (e.g. `1 EUR = 112.00 INR`) to be notified immediately when favorable remittance conditions are met.

### How does the tax engine handle cross-border residency?

Selvam includes specialized calculators for:

- **India Income Tax**: Advance tax installment schedules (Q1 June 15, Q2 Sept 15, Q3 Dec 15, Q4 March 15), capital gains tax categorization (STCG/LTCG), and Section 80C deductions.
- **Netherlands Box 3**: Wealth tax thresholds, deemed return percentages across savings vs investments, and 30% ruling impact modeling.

### How does Selvam track physical gold, sovereign gold bonds (SGB), and bullion?

Selvam fetches live 24-karat and 22-karat spot bullion prices and tracks Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGB) including bi-annual 2.5% coupon credits, capital appreciation, and tax-free capital gains upon RBI redemption.

### How do mortgage amortisation and prepayment simulations work?

Selvam computes monthly EMI deductions, tracks the exact split between interest and principal reduction, and lets you simulate how lump-sum prepayments or incremental EMI hikes shorten your loan tenure and save compound interest.

### How are SIP executions and mutual fund NAVs reconciled?

Selvam automatically matches automated bank debits with mutual fund unit allotments, recording purchase NAV, stamp duty, folio units accumulated, and rolling portfolio XIRR.

### How does Selvam handle document and insurance expiry notifications?

You can store renewal deadlines for passports, term life insurance policies, vehicle insurance, and OCI cards. Selvam delivers proactive alerts at 60-day, 30-day, and 7-day intervals to avoid lapses and penalties.
